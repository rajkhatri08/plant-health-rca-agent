  • The Watch band isn't built yet: it needs equipment-group attribution.
  • During an alert, the marked group is where the detector's reconstruction of the data points when the alert began. It shows where the symptoms appear, not what caused them, and it isn't an instruction to act.
  • The ratio is how far the plant-wide statistic is from its alert limit; above 1 is outside normal operation. An alert needs several samples in a row above 1, and it stays on for a hold time after the ratio drops back, so one disturbance gives one alert.
  • Each point is computed only from data up to that moment: nothing later changes what was shown earlier.
  • The first samples of a stream are Unknown: the detector needs a short warm-up before it scores.
